07 · BEFORE ANYTHING SHIPS
  • Only blue, gold, navy, ink, paper, white and the listed grays
  • At most one gold "look here" per screen or frame
  • Tablet Gothic for everything except mono labels
  • Section numbers match across site, video and slides
  • Charts follow the data viz rules and have a source
  • No GPS audit or Gemini in final copy
  • Real Kent photos with releases
  • Works with keyboard only and with reduced motion
