Students don't lack resources.They lack one place to see them.

03 · How ACE works

Not another platform.A layer inside FlashLine.

  1. Opens from FlashLine or a KSUMobile notificationStudents choose when the daily notification arrives. Kent email is the backup.
  2. Same Kent loginMicrosoft Entra ID single sign-on and MFA. No new account or password.
  3. Reads, never writesEach morning ACE checks four systems with read-only access. It can't change grades, enrollment or records.
  4. Ranks what mattersMicrosoft Copilot turns the day's data into three priorities, with names and IDs removed first.
  5. Sends students back to actEvery item links to its source. Students still submit in Canvas and register in Schedule Builder.

06 · The path to approval

Built to pass Kent's review,not just to demo well.

Any tool used by more than 100 people or touching protected Kent data must clear review before a contract or use. That includes tools built in-house. ACE is designed around four checkpoints.

01Information Security

Security review (HECVAT)

The higher-ed security questionnaire every tool must pass.

  • Entra ID single sign-on + MFA
  • TLS 1.3 and AES-256 encryption
  • Private Azure subnet
  • Audit logging + AI kill switch
02General Counsel + Privacy

FERPA compliance

Student records stay protected under the school official exception.

  • Identifiers removed before AI
  • Zero retention under Microsoft's data terms
  • Respects FERPA directory holds
  • One-click student opt-out
03Digital Accessibility

Accessibility (VPAT)

A conformance report to WCAG 2.1 AA before any pilot.

  • Semantic HTML and React
  • Full keyboard navigation
  • Screen reader announcements
  • 4.5:1 Kent navy and gold contrast
04Registrar + Advising

Data stewardship

A formal request for exact read-only access.

  • Read-only tokens only
  • No degree-rule interpretation
  • Refers students to advisors
  • Sign-off before any data flows

Sources: Kent State University, Pre-purchase product review (n.d.-d); team interview with Kent IT, Nov 13, 2026.

Proof that nudges work

Georgia State's AI reminders cut summer melt by a fifth.

In a randomized trial with more than 7,000 incoming students, those who got proactive AI nudges were less likely to drop out before fall. Fewer than 1% of 50,000 student messages needed staff attention.

08 · User testing

We tested it the waya pilot would.

Each tester finds information for a student two ways: across six mock Kent portals, and with ACE. The order is randomized. Then they rate ACE's picks and fill out a short survey.

  • 01Time to find each task
  • 02Correct answers, current way vs. ACE
  • 03Accuracy of ACE's top three priorities
  • 04Usefulness and trust ratings
  • 05When students want the notification
